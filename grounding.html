Grounding Theory

The theoretical frame: grounding relocated into space. Scaffold for YY to develop. Material in Docs/refs/jun-2026/session_2026-06-30_genre_grounding_plan.md.

1The lens: common ground and grounding

STUB (YY): Clark and Brennan: collaboration runs on common ground, maintained by grounding, the joint work of reaching mutual belief that something is understood well enough for the purpose at hand. Grounding has costs, and those costs shift with the medium.

2The costs, in our setting

Strip the medium down to "a shared space, but never a shared moment" and specific grounding costs spike. They map onto our three gates:

Grounding cost (Clark and Brennan)In shared-space, asynchronous
start-up: register the addresseeno addressee to target; the recipient is unknown
formulation: put it in wordsno indicative gesture; forced to describe an invisible referent
understanding: reconstruct contextlands on a reader who must rebuild where and when it mattered

3Where the theory stops

STUB (YY): two load-bearing assumptions break. (1) No acceptance phase: no live partner to return evidence across a shift gap. (2) Reference and context are assumed within a dialogue between known interlocutors, but here recipient, moment, and place are all unknown at authoring.

4Our proposal: grounding relocated into space

Grounding relocated from a synchronous two-party achievement into a space-anchored, accumulated structure, through three substitutions:

In conversationIn Spatial Memory
choose a recipientanchor to a place
describe the referentthe reader stands where it was said, so deixis is restored across time
accept with a live replya later person acts on it, a deferred, world-mediated acceptance

The synchronous version of this is From Speech-to-Spatial (Kim et al., ISMAR 2026): a live shared view, real-time grounding. They are a spatial Zoom; we are a spatial Slack.

5Boundaries

STUB (YY): is it true common ground (mutual belief), or one-way, stigmergic transfer? Do not assert common ground; the study characterizes where mutual understanding holds and where it is only deferred one-way landing. Stigmergy, distributed cognition, and common information spaces are the alternative lenses for the acceptance side.
