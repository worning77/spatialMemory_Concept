The shape of the paper, at a glance:
Theory (Clark & Brennan grounding: common ground, deixis, grounding cost) → [optional: a small formative typology] → System (a relational Memory Graph + spatial anchoring, on Quest 3) → Evaluation, two legs: technical pipeline success and reliability (quantify the pieces) + a qualitative user study (N ≈ 12–16, confirmatory condition contrast + baseline + NASA-TLX) → Discussion (name the portable concept and the boundaries).

1How the paper argues

We do not study a system to see what it does. We propose a theory and build a system to show it holds. The spine:

Step
Prior theoryClark and Brennan's grounding: collaboration runs on common ground, built by joint work whose cost shifts with the medium.
Our propositiongrounding can be relocated into shared space, across time (§2)
SystemSpatial Memory on Quest 3 instantiates it
Evaluationtwo legs: quantify the pieces (technical) + a qualitative layer that confirms and bounds the theory
Discussionname the portable concept and the boundaries, where it holds and where it is only one-way

This is a concept proposal confirmed by evaluation, not an open "understanding the role of X." The system is simple by design; the theory is the load-bearing contribution.

2The proposition

Grounding, in Clark and Brennan a synchronous two-party achievement, can be relocated into a space-anchored, accumulated structure through three substitutions:

In conversationIn Spatial Memory
choose a recipientanchor to a place
describe the referentspatial co-location: the reader stands where it was said, so deixis is restored across time
accept with a live replya later person acts on it, a deferred, world-mediated acceptance

This is the asynchronous case. From Speech-to-Spatial (Kim et al., ISMAR 2026) does the synchronous one: a live shared view, real-time grounding. In one line, they are a spatial Zoom; we are a spatial Slack.

3How we test it, in grounding terms

We read the study through Clark and Brennan's grounding. First we place the medium: on their eight properties of a medium (copresence, cotemporality, reviewability, and the rest), a shared physical space is a new profile, asynchronous but with copresence of place, which the Framing tab lays out and which predicts our costs. The proposition is then three things; the study confirms each and reports where it breaks, with two kinds of evidence: numbers on the system pieces, read as grounding costs, and interviews on whether grounding was reached.

Thing 1 · the author's cost drops (leaver)

This is the start-up and formulation cost in Clark and Brennan, the cost of finding an addressee and putting a note into words. Test: a hands-busy task; leave a note by in-place voice (our system) versus phone-and-channel (baseline), both wearing the headset. Measure: externalization rate and effort (NASA-TLX, time-off-task). Depth: small, N ≈ 10–12, within-subject.

Thing 2 · the reader's cost is kept low (arriver, the graph)

By least collaborative effort, lowering the author's cost is not enough if it dumps cost on the reader; the Memory Graph exists to keep the reader's understanding cost low. Test: pre-seeded week-long rooms, SM-full versus anchored-only. Two traps: state resolution (is an old warning still live or resolved?) and cross-place routing (a warning left elsewhere must surface here). Measure: trap avoidance, time-to-relevant, overload (NASA-TLX). Depth: N ≈ 12–16, two matched rooms.

Thing 3 · grounding is reached, or not (deferred acceptance)

Grounding completes only with positive evidence that the reader understood to a sufficient criterion; async has no live evidence, so the later person's action stands in for it. Test: interviews on the same arrivers; did they reconstruct the author's meaning, did standing at the place help, where were they unsure, did they do what the author intended. Judge: "grounded" = acted correctly for the intended reason; "broke" = misread, right for the wrong reason, unsure, or mismatched criteria. Depth: deepest here, two coders, focused thematic analysis.

The yardstick, in one line

Two grounding measures sit over these three. Total collaborative cost (author plus reader) should drop and redistribute versus the baseline: least collaborative effort made visible. And grounding reached, the grounded-versus-broke rate, is the outcome. The honest boundary: async removes repair and cannot complete mutual belief, so we report where grounding is only one-way. That boundary is what makes this a grounding paper, not a system test, and it supplies the discussion.

Supporting

Technical evaluation (no participants): structuring accuracy and anchoring reliability on Quest 3. Scenario (filmed): a demonstration that the loop is coherent end to end, not empirical data.

4The study, concretely

Draft protocol. The direction is fixed; specific task steps can change. What is locked is the two-role chain and, for each study, what we record and what we measure. Both studies are in-lab on Quest 3 (passthrough), run with makerspace-experienced people from outside the lab (labmates pilot only).

The chain (why two studies are one pipeline):
Study 1 real utterances (recorded + located) → real LLM segmentation, offline (this is where RQ3 is measured) → a curated frozen seed set (one representative real note per trap + researcher fillers) → identical pre-seeded rooms → Study 2. No live relay — every Study 2 participant walks into the same room, or the conditions do not compare. Every number in the paper traces back to words real participants spoke while working.

Study 1 · the Leaver (capture cost — Thing 1)

Who: N≈10–12, within-subject, ~40 min/session, in-lab. Cover story: "You are the morning shift; someone you do not know takes over this station this afternoon. Do the job; handle anything you notice however you normally would." Recording is never mentioned — leaving a note is optional and costly, exactly like real life.

Conditions (two matched task variants A/B, counterbalanced): SM = speak in place, wizarded (audio + location genuinely recorded, no live pipeline behind it)  vs  Slack = phone in pocket, lab channels available. Both conditions wear the headset (passthrough), so device burden is held constant and the only variable is the capture channel: given the glasses, speak or pull the phone?

Task (no soldering), ~30 min, mild time pressure: collect a draft print from the printer → assemble a bracket at the workbench → wire and clip a connector by hand → stage a logging phone for a 48-hour test. Six planted encounters — three caused by the participant, three discovered; three of them feed the Study 2 traps:

What we record: the spoken notes (audio) and each note's anchor location + timestamp (SM condition); the phone/Slack messages (baseline); per-record time-off-task; a screen/passthrough capture of the session for behavior coding.

What we measure: externalization rate per noticed encounter — a post-task manipulation-check interview ("walk me through what you noticed") separates did not notice from noticed but did not record, and only the latter is the phenomenon; flow cost = time-off-task per record + NASA-TLX; usefulness of what got recorded (blinded third-party rating). Exit novelty check: "in your own lab, would you still have said these?"

Questionnaire instrument (Qualtrics): consent → background → NASA-TLX after each variant → exit questions.

Study 2 · the Arriver (reader cost + grounding reached — Things 2 & 3)

Who: N=12–16, within-subject, ~50 min/session, in-lab. Cover story: "You are the afternoon shift, new to this project. Get up to speed, then complete three jobs."

Conditions (two matched pre-seeded rooms Week A/Week B, counterbalanced): SM-full = graph on (triage, supersession, audience filter, trail)  vs  anchored-only = the ablation: the same notes hanging raw in place, no supersession (the stale nozzle note hangs beside the new one), no filter, no expiry, no triage. Each room holds ~35–40 notes including superseded pairs and notes addressed to other people, so structure has real work to do. Slack is not a Study 2 condition — it was the capture-side baseline; Study 2 isolates the one variable that matters here, structure. This ablation is the most important condition in the paper: the two rooms differ in exactly one thing, so any difference is attributable to the Memory Graph, and it is the data that kills "sticky notes in AR."

What we record: task actions and outcomes (each trap hit vs avoided); time-to-first-relevant-note; T0 triage time; retrieval errors; a screen capture of the session; the exit-interview audio.

What we measure: traps avoided (/3); time-to-relevant and T0 triage time; retrieval errors; perceived overload (NASA-TLX); preference. Then the Thing-3 layer: an interview on the same arrivers — did they reconstruct the author's meaning, did standing at the place help, where were they unsure, did they act as intended — coded grounded (acted correctly for the intended reason) vs broke (misread, right for the wrong reason, unsure, mismatched criteria), two coders, focused thematic analysis.

Questionnaire instrument (Qualtrics): consent → background → NASA-TLX/overload after each room → preference + exit.

Technical evaluation (RQ3, no participants)

Segmentation on the full Study 1 corpus (+ pilot recordings) against gold labels: % correct object/project filing, audience %, supersession detection %, capture-to-card latency. Anchoring: repeated approach trials across sessions and relocalizations on Quest 3 — surfacing reliability % and latency.

Still open (task-level, not direction)
