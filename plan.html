1How the paper argues

We do not study a system to see what it does. We propose a theory and build a system to show it holds. The spine:

Step
Prior theoryClark and Brennan's grounding: collaboration runs on common ground, built by joint work whose cost shifts with the medium.
Our propositiongrounding can be relocated into shared space, across time (§2)
SystemSpatial Memory on Quest Pro instantiates it
Evaluationtwo legs: quantify the pieces (technical) + a qualitative layer that confirms and bounds the theory
Discussionname the portable concept and the boundaries, where it holds and where it is only one-way

This is a concept proposal confirmed by evaluation, not an open "understanding the role of X." The system is simple by design; the theory is the load-bearing contribution.

2The proposition

Grounding, in Clark and Brennan a synchronous two-party achievement, can be relocated into a space-anchored, accumulated structure through three substitutions:

In conversationIn Spatial Memory
choose a recipientanchor to a place
describe the referentspatial co-location: the reader stands where it was said, so deixis is restored across time
accept with a live replya later person acts on it, a deferred, world-mediated acceptance

This is the asynchronous case. From Speech-to-Spatial (Kim et al., ISMAR 2026) does the synchronous one: a live shared view, real-time grounding. In one line, they are a spatial Zoom; we are a spatial Slack.

3How we test it, in grounding terms

We read the study through Clark and Brennan's grounding. First we place the medium: on their eight properties of a medium (copresence, cotemporality, reviewability, and the rest), a shared physical space is a new profile, asynchronous but with copresence of place, which the Framing tab lays out and which predicts our costs. The proposition is then three things; the study confirms each and reports where it breaks, with two kinds of evidence: numbers on the system pieces, read as grounding costs, and interviews on whether grounding was reached.

Thing 1 · the author's cost drops (leaver)

This is the start-up and formulation cost in Clark and Brennan, the cost of finding an addressee and putting a note into words. Test: a hands-busy task; leave a note by in-place voice (our system) versus phone-and-channel (baseline), both wearing the headset. Measure: externalization rate and effort (NASA-TLX, time-off-task). Depth: small, N ≈ 10–12, within-subject.

Thing 2 · the reader's cost is kept low (arriver, the graph)

By least collaborative effort, lowering the author's cost is not enough if it dumps cost on the reader; the Memory Graph exists to keep the reader's understanding cost low. Test: pre-seeded week-long rooms, SM-full versus anchored-only. Two traps: state resolution (is an old warning still live or resolved?) and cross-place routing (a warning left elsewhere must surface here). Measure: trap avoidance, time-to-relevant, overload (NASA-TLX). Depth: N ≈ 12–16, two matched rooms.

Thing 3 · grounding is reached, or not (deferred acceptance)

Grounding completes only with positive evidence that the reader understood to a sufficient criterion; async has no live evidence, so the later person's action stands in for it. Test: interviews on the same arrivers; did they reconstruct the author's meaning, did standing at the place help, where were they unsure, did they do what the author intended. Judge: "grounded" = acted correctly for the intended reason; "broke" = misread, right for the wrong reason, unsure, or mismatched criteria. Depth: deepest here, two coders, focused thematic analysis.

The yardstick, in one line

Two grounding measures sit over these three. Total collaborative cost (author plus reader) should drop and redistribute versus the baseline: least collaborative effort made visible. And grounding reached, the grounded-versus-broke rate, is the outcome. The honest boundary: async removes repair and cannot complete mutual belief, so we report where grounding is only one-way. That boundary is what makes this a grounding paper, not a system test, and it supplies the discussion.

Supporting

Technical evaluation (no participants): structuring accuracy and anchoring reliability on Quest Pro. Scenario (filmed): a demonstration that the loop is coherent end to end, not empirical data.
