1How the paper argues

We do not study a system to see what it does. We propose a theory and build a system to show it holds. The spine:

Step
Prior theoryClark and Brennan's grounding: collaboration runs on common ground, built by joint work whose cost shifts with the medium.
Our propositiongrounding can be relocated into shared space, across time (§2)
SystemSpatial Memory on Quest Pro instantiates it
Evaluationtwo legs: quantify the pieces (technical) + a qualitative layer that confirms and bounds the theory
Discussionname the portable concept and the boundaries, where it holds and where it is only one-way

This is a concept proposal confirmed by evaluation, not an open "understanding the role of X." The system is simple by design; the theory is the load-bearing contribution.

2The proposition

Grounding, in Clark and Brennan a synchronous two-party achievement, can be relocated into a space-anchored, accumulated structure through three substitutions:

In conversationIn Spatial Memory
choose a recipientanchor to a place
describe the referentspatial co-location: the reader stands where it was said, so deixis is restored across time
accept with a live replya later person acts on it, a deferred, world-mediated acceptance

This is the asynchronous case. From Speech-to-Spatial (Kim et al., ISMAR 2026) does the synchronous one: a live shared view, real-time grounding. In one line, they are a spatial Zoom; we are a spatial Slack.

3How we test it

The proposition is three things; the study confirms each and honestly reports where it breaks. Two kinds of evidence: numbers on the system pieces, and interviews on how grounding was experienced.

Thing 1 · speaking in place is cheaper (the leaver)

Test: a hands-busy task with planted observations; leave a note by in-place voice (our system) versus phone-and-channel (baseline). Both wear the headset. Measure: externalization rate, of what was noticed, and effort (NASA-TLX, time-off-task). Depth: small, N ≈ 10–12, within-subject.

Thing 2 · the note reaches the right person and place (the arriver)

Test: pre-seeded week-long rooms; SM-full versus anchored-only (same notes, no structure). Two traps: state resolution (is an old warning still live or already resolved?) and cross-place routing (a warning left elsewhere must surface here). Measure: trap avoidance, time-to-relevant, overload (NASA-TLX). Depth: small to medium, N ≈ 12–16, two matched rooms.

Thing 3 · the later person understands and acts (deferred acceptance)

The new and load-bearing part, and the one only a Slack has. Test: a qualitative layer on the same arrivers; after each note, ask whether they understood the author, whether standing at the place helped, where they were unsure, and whether they did what the author intended. Judge: "accepted" = acted correctly for the note's intended reason; "broke" = misread, right for the wrong reason, unsure, or mismatched criteria. Measure: accepted-versus-broke rate, the types of breakdown, and how re-grounding happened. Depth: deepest here; two coders, focused thematic analysis. This layer is what makes it a grounding paper, not a system test, and it supplies the discussion.

Supporting

Technical evaluation (no participants): structuring accuracy (object and project filing, audience, supersession) and anchoring reliability on Quest Pro. Scenario (filmed): a demonstration that the loop is coherent end to end, not empirical data.
