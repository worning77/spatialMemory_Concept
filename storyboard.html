Thread 1 · the incident (Sam + Priya)

Monday, Sam runs the solvent-extraction step for Project A at the fume-hood and leaves a voice note anchored to the hood, referencing Iris's 2x concentration note. A seal fails: a minor hand injury, and contamination spreads to the counter and shelf. Priya is paged, closes the corner, and leaves a zone-level anchor, "Contamination, corner sealed, check with me first," which overrides every note already anchored inside that zone. Wednesday, Priya walks the zone with Dae-ho, relocates items, and posts a superseding note at the old shelf position pointing to the new location. Thursday, Facilities finishes decontamination and Priya closes the zone note, so it expires.

Thread 2 · Project A (Iris)

Monday afternoon, Iris checks the extraction and receives Priya's zone note plus a system-generated causal card, auto-threaded by the graph: Sam injured, so the procedure is incomplete, so the next step is blocked. Because this is scheduling information, she logs her own note to the project rather than the place: the extraction re-run is pushed to next week, pending Sam. Friday, that re-run note is one of exactly two cards routed to Sam the following week.

Thread 3 · Project B (Renata)

Renata was not involved in Monday's incident. Tuesday she checks in and gets only the one card relevant to her dependency: Sam is unavailable this week, and the Wednesday slot is open. No full incident report. She re-sequences her own schedule, moving calibration and slotting in an antibody-panel run, and leaves a note at her bench. Friday, her rescheduled-calibration note is the second of the two cards routed to Sam.

Thread 4 · Project C (Dae-ho)

Tuesday afternoon, Dae-ho gets no causal or incident narrative, only the zone note itself, because his equipment's problem is spatial, the wrong shelf, not procedural. He leaves a note at the same anchor: he needs his models out before Thursday's review. Wednesday, Priya and Dae-ho clear the zone together, and his equipment gets a fresh anchor at its new location, the cart by the door. Project C never resurfaces in Sam's view, because nothing in Sam's path touches Dae-ho's models.

Thread 5 · Sam's return

Thursday, Sam is back and mobile. He revisits the hood and gets the full replay in sequence: his own Monday note, Priya's containment note, Iris's reschedule, and the clearance note. Four notes, from four people, at one location, ordered as they happened. Friday, checking "anything for me?", Sam receives only the two notes that route to him personally, Iris's and Renata's, with Project C staying entirely outside his view.
