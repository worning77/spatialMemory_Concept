Sam (Project A)

Monday he preps the A capture and seeds five broadcast notes plus one project note, each costing a breath, then is offsite. Thursday's triage hands him three cards, and the two that spent Wednesday ten feet apart and invisible to each other's readers, Dae-ho's bump and Iris's "capture done", finally sit side by side and say exactly what to check. He returns the watch, recalibrates, repairs the capture with Iris, freezes the lesson into a read-first rule, and archives the handled card.

Iris (Project A, new this week)

New to the project, brought in to run the capture. Wednesday the trail catches her up in ten minutes and the frame-rate card finds her at the rig. She reads a true Monday note, reasonably takes it as current, confirms it, and captures: silently wrong, through no fault of hers, because the correcting card was parked in a queue she is not allowed to see. Thursday she re-captures clean, two days and one wasted session late.

Renata (Project B)

Tuesday, Sam's note turns an occupied-looking machine into a five-minute swap: deliberate, finished, permission granted, and the 0.4 is in the tray. Her sentence stacks on top of his, so the printer shows what is true now. Friday she prints again without a thought, which is the point.

Dae-ho (Project C)

Tuesday the empty spot tells him where his box went; he retrieves it and archives the note. Then he clips a rig camera and does the polite old-media thing: tells the owner, by name. The card waits two days in Sam's queue. Friday the room pays him back in kind: a stranger's warning meets him at the soldering station before he touches the frayed cable, four hours after it was spoken.

Priya (Project D)

Tuesday the empty watch slot answers everything, who has it, why, and that a dark screen means working; her reply negotiates a Thursday return that closes by action alone. Friday one breath at the door becomes two cards in two places, one of them a warning; she signs the placement with a glance back and is gone.

The full week, in prose

The same week as a narrative. Story text is story; concept commentary lives in italic parentheses. Note tags follow each spoken note.

Setting. A shared maker lab: one Bambu P2S printer, a hand-capture rig in the corner (a cube frame over the capture bench, a camera at each of its four corners), a shelf of shared test devices, a soldering station, a pegboard tool wall, and five workstations. Several projects share the printer, the rig, and the shelf, but the people rarely overlap in time.

Monday. Sam seeds the room.

Sam is setting up a Project A hand-capture session for later in the week. The capture needs a stiff custom mount to hold the test device steady, so at the Bambu he pulls the standard 0.4 nozzle, threads in carbon-fiber filament from the external spool, and drops in a hardened 0.6. He does it properly: printer menu set to 0.6 hardened, his slicer profile switched to match. He starts the print and says into the room, without breaking stride: "0.6 hardened and CF are in for the A mount. Once my job's done, feel free to swap back to 0.4 and PLA; the 0.4 is in the tray under the bench." (object memory · printer · broadcast)

(Concept link: the P2S guards its own numbers and will refuse a job sliced for the wrong nozzle, but a menu field holds state, not intent. Only the note says the 0.6 is deliberate, that swapping back is allowed, and where the 0.4 physically is.)

While the mount prints, one more thought lands, and this one he files to the project rather than to a place: "For whoever runs the A capture, set the cameras to 40, they come up at 60; the reconstruction runs at 40 and wants all four in step." (project memory · Project A · project members)

(Concept link: routing. Sam cannot know where or when the capture will run, so the note follows the work instead of a place.)

He crosses to the capture rig, a cube frame over the capture bench with a camera looking in from each corner. The week's capture needs the cameras calibrated, so he runs the calibration himself and packs the board away. Nothing about the rig shows what he just did: from across the room it looks exactly as it did yesterday. So he says: "Recalibrated the rig this morning." (object memory · rig · broadcast)

(Concept link: calibration is invisible state, the kind a camera watching the room could never recover. The note is true and grounded to Sam's own criterion; it carries no expiry date, and nothing in the medium asks him for one.)

The capture bench is cluttered: someone's personal box, used and never returned, is sitting where the test device needs to go. Sam sets it on the shelf and leaves a note at the empty spot: "Box is on the shelf, needed the bench clear." (object memory · bench spot · broadcast) Then, on his way out, he takes the shared watch from the device shelf and sets it on his own desk; at the shelf he leaves: "Took the watch, it's running a log for me this week, please leave it." (object memory · shelf · broadcast) And he is gone, offsite until Thursday.

(Concept link: five sentences, four anchored to places, one to a project, none naming a recipient, because Sam has no idea who prints, captures, or comes looking for the box or the watch next. All five are broadcast; the week's only directed note, and its only failure, is still to come.)

Tuesday. The fan-out, and one small collision.

Renata, on Project B, comes to print a fine calibration plate. She lifts Sam's finished mount off the plate and sets it beside the printer, and as she steps up to the machine Sam's note is already there: 0.6 hardened and CF are in, the job they were for is done, she is free to swap back, and the 0.4 is in the tray under the bench. She swaps the nozzle, hangs the CF back on the rack, loads her PLA, prints, and says: "0.4 and PLA back in, CF's on the rack." Her sentence stacks on top of Sam's, and the printer shows what is true now. (object memory · printer · broadcast · supersedes Sam's note)

(Concept link: the P2S would have caught the raw diameter mismatch on its own, but a machine can only guard its numbers; the note carries intent and permission, whose it was, that it is finished, that swapping is allowed, where the 0.4 lives. Without it: a channel question aimed at someone offsite, or swapping blind. The supersession is state-not-log in action: the next person sees the current answer, not a history.)

Priya, on Project D, needs the shared watch for a device test. She goes where the watch lives, and the shelf slot is empty; in its place sits Sam's note: took the watch, it is running a log, please leave it. So the watch is not lost, not borrowed by a stranger, and not idle on a desk by accident. She needs it Thursday, so she replies into the empty slot: "Need it Thursday 10am, leaving it on the shelf works." Her reply hangs under Sam's note, waiting with it. (reply · shelf · Replies_To Sam's note)

(Concept link: the note turns a missing object into an answered question, who has it, why, and that its dark screen means working, not idle. The reply is asynchronous negotiation: she cannot reach Sam live, so she attaches her request to the thread, anchored where the watch will return, and the exchange completes across two days without either person interrupting the other.)

Dae-ho, on Project C, comes for his box and finds the bench spot where he left it empty. The note at the spot points him to the shelf. He takes his box back and taps the note archived: handled, no need to greet the next passerby. (archive: handled, not deleted) Carrying the box between the benches, he swings a little wide, and a corner of it catches one of the rig cameras. He stops and looks. The frame sits square; all four cameras point where they point; nothing looks different. But the rig is covered in Sam's notes, so it is plainly Sam's setup, and telling the owner seems like the responsible thing: "Sam, heads up, I clipped one of the rig cameras with a box just now. Looks fine to me, but you'd know better." The name at the front of the sentence does the addressing: the card files itself to Sam. Sam is offsite until Thursday. The card waits. (object memory · rig · directed: Dae-ho → Sam)

(Concept link: the week's only directed note, and its only mistake, made politely. "Whoever uses the rig next" is unknowable, which makes this encounter-class information; Dae-ho addresses it as episode-class, to the person instead of the place, the channel habit carried into the new medium. The system does exactly what it was told: delivers the card to its declared audience, a queue that will not be read before Thursday.)

(Fact check for the day: every exchange today moved facts the room could verify, a nozzle in a machine, a log running, a box on a shelf. Facts are the easy cargo. What is parked in Sam's queue is also a fact; the only thing wrong with it is who is allowed to see it.)

Wednesday. Every light is green.

Iris is new to Project A this week, brought in to run the capture while Sam is away, and she has never touched his setup. She asks the room for Sam's trail, and it walks her through his Monday in order: the printer, where his nozzle note sits folded behind Renata's and the finished mount is waiting beside the machine; the bench he cleared for the capture; the shelf and its watch note. Ten minutes, and she has what would otherwise have been a scramble of messages, or would have gone unasked. She picks up the mount on the way. (trail · query over Sam's notes, in time order)

(Concept link: person is not a stored type but a query over the record; the trail is that query rendered as a path. Sam recorded nothing for her; the tour is assembled from the five sentences he left for other reasons.)

At the rig a purple card surfaces for her, one that was never spoken here: set the cameras to 40, they come up at 60, the reconstruction wants all four in step. Sam said it at the printer and filed it to the project; it surfaces now because she is a Project A member, standing at the capture rig, doing the work the sentence is about. She dials the cameras down to 40. (project memory · routed: spoken at the printer, surfaced at the rig · project members)

(Concept link: routing, the one thing a place alone cannot do. A project note travels with the work: it fires when a member's activity and place match its content, so it surfaces exactly once, for exactly the person running the capture, and never as noise for Renata or Dae-ho.)

Then Sam's note on the rig itself: "Recalibrated the rig this morning." Monday, says the card. A calibration holds until someone changes something, and no note she can see says anyone has; the frame sits square, four cameras pointing where they point. So the rig is ready, and she taps the note confirmed, the way she would acknowledge any handoff. On the record, Sam's note now carries a checkmark with her name on it. (confirm · acknowledgment on Sam's rig note)

She bolts the mount to the bench plate, seats the test device, and runs the captures through the afternoon. Four live views, all tracking, no warnings, and by early evening she is done. She racks the device, and on her way out files one more sentence to the project: "A capture is done, shot at 40, raw files are on the server." Then she is gone. (project memory · Project A · project members)

(Concept link: the trap, laid out. Since yesterday afternoon Dae-ho's card has been anchored to this same rig, ten feet from where she stood, invisible to her because its author chose a recipient instead of a place: a hidden profile with the pooling channel cut. One corner camera answers from a slightly different place than Monday's calibration remembers, and her data is subtly, silently off, the kind of wrong you catch days later in processing.)

(Concept link: count the green lights. Delivered, read, confirmed with her name on the checkmark, and now a completion report, the relevant next turn, positive evidence of an understanding that did not happen. Every signal this medium can carry has come back green; whether the meaning survived the gap is the one signal it cannot carry. Her reasoning was faultless, and she committed without hesitating, because the note was true, the rig looked ready, and there was no one to ask. Delivery worked; grounding did not. To Sam, states 0 through 3 are indistinguishable behind that checkmark, until tomorrow.)

Thursday. The repair, two days late.

Sam is back Thursday morning, and before he reaches his desk he asks the room the question everyone asks it: anything for me? Three cards. Priya's reply under his watch note: needs it at ten, the shelf works. Dae-ho's card from Tuesday, patient under its little clock: clipped one of the rig cameras, looks fine, you'd know better. And Iris's report to the project: capture done, shot at 40, raw files on the server. (triage · for_me: a reply, a directed card, a project report)

(Concept link: pooling, finally. The two cards that spent Wednesday ten feet apart and invisible to each other's readers now sit side by side for the one person allowed to see both, and together they say exactly what to check. The record did its job for its declared audience; the declaration was the mistake, and it cost two days.)

He drops the watch back on its shelf slot on the way, and Priya's request closes without either of them ever saying another word about it. (deferred acceptance, clean: a later person acts, the world carries the answer)

At the rig he checks the corner camera Dae-ho named, and it sits a degree or two shy of where Monday's calibration remembers it. His own note is still pinned there, wearing Iris's checkmark. He re-runs the board, pulls Iris's raw files, and there it is: every take subtly skewed, valid-looking and wrong. He finds Iris, and they trace it together, the bumped camera, the true note, the reasonable reading. She re-captures in the afternoon on the fresh calibration, and this time the data comes out clean. One session wasted, one repair delivered two days after the fault it repairs. (re-calibration · re-capture)

(Concept link: the checkmark sting: the acknowledgment was real, the understanding was not, and behind that green mark Sam could not tell the difference until the data did. The repair itself is the ledger's last row made visible: the medium relocated the repair into the record, but it could not relocate the immediacy, so the cost of the missing repair turn is paid late, and by the wrong person.)

Before he leaves the rig, Sam turns the lesson into structure. "Before any capture, re-run the board. A calibration is only as fresh as the last touch on the rig." The sentence has prerequisite language in it, and the system hears it: it offers to put this note ahead of the capture workflow, and Sam confirms. From now on, anyone standing at this rig about to capture, newcomer or not, meets this sentence first, before the frame-rate card, before anything. (object memory · rig · broadcast · Depends_On: surfaces before all capture notes)

(Concept link: dependency, the last of the three links, born as a repaired misunderstanding frozen into reading order. The next Iris cannot walk Wednesday's path, not because she will be more careful, but because the room now refuses to show her the second step before the first. A rule the team learned once, accruing to the space itself.)

He taps Dae-ho's card archived: handled. The thanks can wait until he next runs into him. (archive · Dae-ho's card)

(Concept link: the lesson of the week's one failure is one sentence: encounter-class information belongs to whoever comes next, and only the place knows who that is. Address the place, not the person.)

Friday. One breath, two places.

Priya wraps her device test late morning and heads out for the weekend, hands full, talking as she goes, one breath: "Heads up, the watch drains way faster than spec when it's logging, charge it before a long session. And the USB-C on the soldering station is fraying near the plug, someone's going to burn a finger." The room sorts the breath as she speaks, and as she reaches the door it asks her to look back: two cards hang where it put them, a usage note at the watch's shelf slot, an amber warning at the soldering station. Right places, right words. "Yes," she says, and is gone. (segmentation: one utterance → two anchored cards · shelf: object, broadcast, info · soldering station: object, broadcast, warning)

(Concept link: two topics, two places, one breath; the splitting, anchoring, and urgency tagging are the system's work, and the glance back is the author's, because nothing enters the shared record unsigned, and the thing being signed is precisely the placement. Audience and urgency are different axes: both cards are broadcast, anyone may see them; the warning differs in when and how loudly, first in line for whoever approaches the station, never at the door for people who come to print.)

Dae-ho comes in after lunch to solder headers for Project C. He has not touched the bench yet when the warning surfaces, ahead of everything else at the station, ahead even of the station's old standing note about solder and spare tips in the second drawer: fraying near the plug. He turns the cable over, and the copper is showing. There is a spare in the drawer; he swaps it, and says so: "Swapped the USB-C, old one's in the e-waste bin." His sentence stacks on top of Priya's warning, and the station shows what is true now. (urgency surfacing: warning precedes the station's info notes · supersede: warning → fixed, state current)

(Concept link: urgency reorders the place's answer, safety before status; and the warning's whole life, spoken, surfaced, acted on, superseded, runs its course in half a day without the two people ever meeting. On Tuesday Dae-ho addressed a person and the information waited two days; today a stranger addressed the place, and it reached him in four hours. No note is ever bare-deleted: a broadcast leaves the stage by supersession, by a named archive, or by expiry, and a wrong warning would go the same way, superseded by "checked it, it's fine," signed.)

Renata stops by mid-afternoon to run one more plate. The printer shows one card, hers, from Tuesday, still true. She loads, prints, leaves. The thought about nozzles does not occur to her, which is the point. (state at a glance: supersession holding)

Some Monday soon, someone none of them has met will walk to the rig to run a capture. Before the frame rate, before anything, the room will hand them one sentence: re-run the board first. The week is over. What it learned is not.

After the week: the room, remembered

This block pairs with the closing figure: the room at week's end, every stack fanned open.

Fan every stack open and the whole week is still hanging in the room. At the printer, Renata's card stands current, Sam's Monday sentence peeking out gray behind it. At the rig, the read-first rule leads, the purple frame-rate card queued behind it for whoever on Project A captures next; folded beneath them, Sam's Monday calibration note, gray now and still wearing Iris's green checkmark, and Dae-ho's archived envelope card tucked into the same history. At the soldering station, Dae-ho's fix stands on top of Priya's amber-edged warning, the old tips note beneath both. The watch's shelf slot holds its closed thread, Sam's note and Priya's reply folded together; the bench spot's box note sits archived, invisible until someone asks. Nothing was deleted all week: the room shows state on top and keeps the log underneath.

(The week in one paragraph: Monday distributed five sentences at the cost of a breath each. Tuesday, facts crossed the gap cleanly, and the one directed card began to wait. Wednesday, a true note met a reasonable inference at the boundary: every deliverable signal green, grounding lost. Thursday, the repair arrived two days late, and became structure. Friday, one breath became two places, and a warning lived out its whole life between strangers. Pinning notes in place is the easy half; supersede, route, filter, and order, the Memory Graph, is why a room can hold a week and still hand each person only theirs. That structure is exactly what the evaluation removes to measure.)

逐日解析 · 中文工作版

先解释术语和英文缩写，再逐天给故事与每个 beat 的功能。与上方英文定稿逐段对应。

术语表

术语解释
con全名就是 "pros and cons" 里的 con（拉丁语 contra，反对），中文即不利因素 / 反对理由。出处是 Convertino 疏散实验的材料设计：每个角色私有 9 条关于各 shelter 的 "cons"，最优解只有把三方的 cons 拼起来才看得见。在我们的语境里它泛化为"判定相关信息"：做对一件事所需的信息被拆成两半，一半在 note 里，另一半在房间里（另一张卡、一个机器状态）。
criterion全名 grounding criterion（接地判据 / 理解的充分性判据），Clark & Brennan 1991 p.129：双方相互相信，听者已把说话人的意思理解到"足以满足当前目的"的程度。理解从不需要完美，只需要够用；而"够用"相对于目的和受众（p.136），读者变了判据就变了。
hidden profile隐藏剖面，Stasser & Titus 1985：每方的信息都真实但不完整，客观最优解只有池化（pooling）分布的信息才能到达。我们的两方版本：Sam 的 note 持一半，房间（Dae-ho 停在 Sam 队列里的卡）持另一半，Iris 池化则成、池化被切断则败。
positive evidence / acknowledgment积极理解证据 / 确认。C&B pp.131–133 的三种积极证据：acknowledgment（确认，最弱）、relevant next turn（相关的下一步）、continued attention（持续注意）。行动正确属于更强的 demonstration（Clark & Schaefer 1989）。关键性质：弱证据可以在没有理解时照常发生 —— 周三的四盏绿灯全是这一条。
states 0–3理解的四个状态（p.130）：0 没注意 / 1 注意到没听清 / 2 听清没理解 / 3 理解了。p.146：非同时媒介里作者看不见对方在哪个 state —— 绿色对勾背后，Sam 无从分辨。
fact 层 vs inference 层Dillenbourg & Traum 2006 白板实验：事实贴上持久介质后口头确认率 37%→6%（持久性替代事实层 grounding），推断层共识仍需对话。预测：事实型 note（喷嘴、log、box）稳过，推断型（"校准过"→"现在仍有效"）才是 trap。
silent commitment沉默执行，Yamashita 2009：修复通道被堵时，人不举手报告困惑，按自己的理解直接执行。Iris 的不迟疑是规律不是粗心。
cross-redundancy跨人冗余，Dillenbourg 的失败指标：后来者重做前人已确立的事。周四 Sam 重跑标定、重核状态 = 它的舞台版，也是 Study 2 的过程测量。
supersession顶替：新卡叠在旧卡上，地点显示当前状态，历史折叠在下。理论身份 = C&B revisability（p.141）扩展到发送之后。
segmentation切分：一段跨话题的口述被系统拆成多张各自锚定的卡（周五 Priya 的一口气 → 设备架 + 焊台两张卡）。切分、锚定、标级是系统的活；出门前回头看一眼落点、说一声 yes 是作者的签名 —— 共享记录里没有未签名的卡。
urgency（两级）info / warning 两级（本项目不设 blocking）。它与 audience 正交：audience 答"谁有资格看"，urgency 答"到了地方之后谁先说话"。warning 仍是走近才出现，只是排在该地点一切内容之前。退场无特殊通道：所有广播卡只有三种退场 —— supersede（状态变了）、archive（署名的"已处理"）、expire（persistence 到期），没有裸删除。
episode-class vs encounter-classepisode：收件人已知、内容有界 → channel/directed 是正确媒介。encounter：下一个读者未知 → 只能交给地点。周二 Dae-ho 的错误 = 把 encounter 错当 episode 处理；周五同类信息交给地点，四小时到达 vs 两天等待。
delivery vs grounding送达与接地的分离：delivery = 信息到达；grounding = 意义被正确理解且有证据闭环。周三的设计就是把两者拉到最开。

周一 —— 播种

故事：Sam 为本周的 A 组手部捕捉做准备。打印硬质支架：换 0.6 硬化喷嘴 + 碳纤维，打印机菜单和切片 profile 都规矩地改好，留言"0.6 和 CF 在机上，我的任务跑完后随便换回 0.4/PLA，0.4 在台下托盘里"。打印时想起一句，归入项目："跑 A capture 的人，把相机设到 40，开机默认是 60，重建管线按 40 跑。"到 rig（罩在捕捉台上的四角相机 frame）跑了标定 —— 从房间任何角度看 rig 和昨天毫无区别 —— 留言"今早重新标定了 rig"。清理捕捉台：把别人没归位的个人 box 挪上架子、空位留言；顺手从设备架拿走共享手表放自己桌上、架子空位留言"在给我跑 log，请勿动"。离开，周四才回。

功能：五条全是 broadcast，全周唯一的 directed（也是唯一的失败）尚未登场。打印机段演 state-vs-intent（机器守得住参数、装不下意图/许可/位置）；rig 留言埋下双伏笔 —— criterion（对 Sam 的目的完全达标）与 freshness（没有有效期，媒介也不索要）。

周二 —— 扇出，和一次小碰撞

故事：Renata 来打精细板，取下 Sam 打好的支架放在打印机旁；note 已在机器上：0.6 是故意的、任务完了、可以换、0.4 在托盘。她换头、打印、留言顶替。Priya 需要手表，先去它住的架子 —— 空位上 Sam 的 note 解释了一切；她需要周四用，在空位下回帖"周四早十点要用，放回架子即可"。Dae-ho 按空位 note 找回自己的 box 并 archive；抱着 box 拐弯时碰到一台角相机 —— 停下看，frame 端正、四台相机各指其位、毫无异样，但 rig 上全是 Sam 的 note，告诉主人才是负责："Sam，提醒一下，我刚用箱子碰了一台 rig 相机，看着没事，但你比我懂。"名字一出口，addressing 就定了：卡片进 Sam 的队列。Sam 周四才回。卡片等着。

功能：Renata = pooling 成功的正面版（机器一半信息 + note 一半信息）；Priya = reply 做异步协商（周四靠行动闭合）；Dae-ho = archive 展示 + 全周唯一 directed 卡 —— encounter-class 被礼貌地错当 episode-class，系统忠实执行了他的选择。当天三次交换全是事实层：facts are the easy cargo。

周三 —— 灯全是绿的

故事：Iris 本周新加入 A 组、替 Sam 跑 capture、从没碰过他的设置。走 Sam 的 trail 十分钟补完他的周一，顺路取走支架。rig 前浮现一张从未在此说出的紫卡（相机设置，因为她是 A 组成员、站在捕捉设备前、正做这件事），她把相机调到 40。然后读到 rig 上的"今早重新标定了"（卡片显示周一）：标定在有人动过之前一直有效，而她看得见的任何 note 都没说有人动过 —— 于是 rig 就绪，她点了确认。装支架、放设备、录一下午：四路画面全部正常、零警告，傍晚收工，给项目留言"A capture 完成，40fps 拍完，raw 已上传"。走人。

功能：trail = person-as-query；routing = 成员+活动+地点匹配（对 Renata/Dae-ho 是噪音的卡，只对她出现一次）。Trap 全景：Dae-ho 的卡从昨天下午起就锚在这台 rig 上、离她三米，只因作者选了收件人而对她不可见 —— 池化通道被切断的 hidden profile。四盏绿灯：送达、已读、已确认、完成汇报（relevant next turn，为一个没有发生的理解产生的积极证据）。她的推理无可挑剔、且沉默执行 —— 失败全归媒介，不归任何人。

周四 —— 迟到两天的修复

故事：Sam 回来，triage 给他三张卡：Priya 的回帖、Dae-ho 等了两天的卡、Iris 的完成汇报。顺路把表放回架子（Priya 的请求靠行动闭合，双方再没说一个字）。到 rig 查 Dae-ho 说的那台相机 —— 比周一的标定记忆偏了一两度；自己的旧 note 还挂着 Iris 的对勾。重跑标定板、拉出 raw 数据：每条都微偏、看着有效、全是错的。找到 Iris 一起追查（被碰的相机、真实的 note、合理的解读），下午她在新标定上重录，干净。然后 Sam 把教训写成结构："任何 capture 前先重跑标定板" —— 句子里有前置语言，系统提议排在 capture 流程之首，他确认。最后 archive 掉 Dae-ho 的卡；道谢等下次碰面再说。

功能：triage = pooling 终于发生（周三隔了三米互不可见的两张卡并排出现）；手表 = deferred acceptance 的干净正例（与周三对照）；对勾之刺 = 绿勾背后 states 0–3 不可分辨；重跑重核 = cross-redundancy 上演；修复迟到两天 = ledger 最后一行（repair 可以搬进媒介，即时性搬不回来）；Depends_On 诞生 = 一次修好的误解凝固成阅读顺序，下一个 Iris 结构上无法重走周三。

周五 —— 一口气，两个地方

故事：Priya 收工过周末，两手占满、边走边说一口气：手表录 log 时掉电远超标称、长时间用前充满；焊台 USB-C 插头处磨破皮，早晚烫到人。房间边听边拆：设备架一张使用提示卡、焊台一张琥珀色 warning。走到门口，系统请她回头看一眼落点 —— 位置对、话对 —— "Yes"，走人。午后 Dae-ho 来焊排针，还没碰工作台，warning 先于焊台的一切（包括那张"焊锡和备用烙铁头在第二个抽屉"的常驻旧卡）浮现；翻开线一看铜丝已露，换上备用线，留言顶替。下午 Renata 再来打一块板：打印机上只有她自己周二那张卡，仍然为真；装料、打印、走人，喷嘴这件事根本没进入她的脑子。不久后的某个周一，一个五人都不认识的人会走到 rig 前 —— 在帧率之前、在一切之前，房间会先递上一句：先重跑标定板。这一周结束了；它学到的东西没有。

功能：segmentation = capture 成本主张的完成式（一口气 ≈ 两张异地卡，签名 = 回头一瞥）；urgency = warning 压过常驻 info 卡（层级只有在压过别人时才可见）；warning 的一生（说出→浮现→执行→被顶替）半天走完、两人从未见面 —— 四小时 vs Dae-ho 自己周二的两天，address-the-place 的定量对照；Renata 的"想都没想" = supersession 价值的最低调证词。

一周之后（总结块，配收官全景图）

把所有卡堆摊开，整周还挂在房间里：打印机上 Renata 的卡现行、Sam 的灰卡在后面探头；rig 上 read-first 规则领跑、紫色相机设置卡排队、下面折着仍戴着绿勾的周一标定卡和 archive 掉的信封卡；焊台上修复卡叠着琥珀边的 warning、再下面是常驻旧卡；手表架位折着闭合的 thread；box 空位的卡归档待查。一周之内没有任何东西被删除：状态在上，历史在下。一句话总览：周一分发（一口气一条），周二事实过河 + directed 卡开始等待，周三真 note 撞上合理推断（灯全绿、grounding 丢失），周四修复迟到并凝固成规则，周五一口气变两地 + 一条 warning 走完一生。钉住 note 是容易的那一半；顶替、路由、过滤、排序（Memory Graph）是评估拿掉来测量的那一半。
