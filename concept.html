Spatial Memory

Concept, mechanism, and interaction in condensed form. Full text and figures are in the Full Doc.

Why it matters: this is grounding, how people reach shared understanding, relocated from a live conversation into a shared physical space that different people visit at different times. The Framing tab has the theory; this tab has the design, and every design choice below carries an old link: the element of Clark & Brennan (1991, page numbers) it substitutes for, restores, or is priced by.

1Concept

Spatial Memory closes a four-step loop: a worker captures a judgment by speaking in place at low effort, the system anchors it, structures it in a Memory Graph, and surfaces its current state to the next person.

Each loop step performs one grounding substitution. In a live conversation the speaker chooses a recipient, describes a referent, and reads a reply that confirms understanding; Spatial Memory substitutes a spatial, accumulated equivalent for each.

Grounding moveSubstitutionLoop stepOld link (C&B)
choose a recipientanchor to a place, no addresseeanchorstart-up and speaker-change costs are what price out addressing (pp. 143–145)
describe the referentspatial co-location: the reader stands where it was said, so deixis is restored across timesurfacecontext is present, not imagined from words (p. 143); indicative reference regains its referent (pp. 137, 145)
accept with a live replydeferred acceptance: a later person acts on the notestructure + surfacethe acceptance phase (p. 130); async states 0–3 are invisible to the author (p. 146)

The third substitution is load-bearing and fragile: a deferred action is not the mutual belief a live reply secures, so grounding can complete only one-way. That boundary is what the evaluation tests, not something the design hides.

It carries encounter-class information: low value alone, tied to a physical thing, with no known reader when spoken. Today's channels price this out, so it is lost. Known-recipient, bounded messages, which are episode-class, stay on Slack.

Every note is one of two memory types:

TypeAnchored toOutlivesExample
Object memorya placeany project"0.6 nozzle is in" on the printer
Project memorya project, spanning placesnothing; dies with the project"shoot the A capture at 120fps" follows the project from the printer to the rig

Person and time are not types but queries over the graph: whose trail, addressed to whom, since when.

Four properties follow, each realizing a substitution: Anchored (notes attach to the place spoken), Accumulated (the room builds up over time, from many people and projects), Layered (persistence, audience, urgency), and Navigable (versioned, filterable, traversable, not a pile of pins).

Low effort, not no effort. Every note is a deliberate utterance: no ambient inference, no gaze or dwell logging, no telemetry. The system removes only the addressing effort, recipient, referent, and app switch, never authorship. That is also the privacy stance: capture of chosen speech, not surveillance.

2Memory Graph

The structuring step is a small typed store, in effect an advanced filter over what a room accumulates. Read against the Framing tab, it is not a data model at all: it is the constraints the medium lacks, re-imposed as structure. Each mechanism has an old link:

MechanismJobOld link (C&B 1991)
Supersedesstate, not log: a place shows what is true nowrevisability (p. 141) extended past sending: the record itself is revised; repair moves into the medium instead of waiting for a costly other-repair (p. 145)
Depends_Onprerequisites surface first, "read this first"sequentiality: turns cannot get out of sequence (p. 141), which every async medium loses
Replies_Tothreads that span days and peopleinitiation of the relevant next turn, the second form of positive evidence (p. 132)
Confirmed_byone-tap "seen, will comply"acknowledgments, the first form of positive evidence (p. 131); makes states 0–3 visible to the author (p. 146)
Audiencebroadcast, directed, or project; each arriver hears only what is for themutterances designed for their addressees (p. 134; Clark & Murphy 1982)
Routinga project memory follows the work to its next placethe recipient substitution generalized: when no place can stand for "whoever continues the work," the project does
Triage + salience"anything for me?" returns a few ordered items; the rest stays quietthe grounding criterion: understanding "sufficient for current purposes" (p. 129), and the criterion is purpose-relative (p. 136), so notes expire when purposes end
Accumulation + historythe reviewable record behind every current statereviewability (p. 141); groups ground onto a persistent medium (Dillenbourg & Traum 2006)

Sequentiality returns as ordering, revisability as supersession, reviewability as accumulation, positive evidence as confirm and reply. The value is the graph's position in the loop, not the data model, which a plain table could hold. The hard part is building it from messy in-flow speech.
