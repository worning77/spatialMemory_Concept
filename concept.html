Spatial Memory

Concept, mechanism, and interaction in condensed form. Full text and figures are in the Full Doc.

Why it matters: this is workspace awareness carried across the temporal gap: the room itself keeps each arriver's picture of what happened current and correct. The Framing tab has the theory (the arriver's questions, the severed channels, the cost ledger); this tab has the design, and every mechanism below carries a theory link: which link of the chain it serves, which arriver's question it answers, or which cost it drives down.

1Concept

Spatial Memory closes a four-step loop: a worker captures a judgment by speaking in place at low effort, the system anchors it, structures it in a Memory Graph, and surfaces its current state to the next person.

The loop's theoretical job is a graft. A note's content is intentional communication: deliberate, authored, able to carry intent and dependencies that no artifact's appearance reveals. Its delivery is feedthrough: anchored in the place, it persists after the actor leaves and reaches whoever walks up next, on the only channel that survives the temporal gap (the Framing tab's figure). Each loop step serves one piece of that graft:

Loop stepTheory jobTheory link
Capturedrive the author-side costs to zero at the moment of encounterthe cost ledger's design brief: formulation, production, start-up
Anchorgraft the utterance onto the channel that survives the actor's absencefeedthrough: the note persists in the place, like a state the artifact gives off
Structurekeep the record true as the room changesawareness maintenance (G&G, p. 416); the Memory Graph, §2
Surfaceanswer the arriver's questions at the thing, at the moment they matterhow did this come to be in this state, when, who (the past elements)

It carries encounter-class information: low value alone, tied to a physical thing, with no known reader when spoken. Today's channels price this out, so it is lost. Known-recipient, bounded messages, which are episode-class, stay on Slack.

Every note is one of two memory types:

TypeAnchored toOutlivesExample
Object memorya placeany project"0.6 nozzle is in" on the printer
Project memorya project, spanning placesnothing; dies with the project"set the A-capture cameras to 40" follows the project from the printer to the rig

Person and time are not types but queries over the graph: whose trail, addressed to whom, since when. The anchor must be a place or a project because the place is what the arriver walks up to, the feedthrough carrier; the author is metadata on the note, which is exactly what makes trail and presence-history queries possible.

Four properties follow, each serving one link of the chain: Anchored (notes attach to the place spoken), Accumulated (the room builds up over time, from many people and projects), Layered (persistence, audience, urgency), and Navigable (versioned, filterable, traversable, not a pile of pins).

Low effort, not no effort. Every note is a deliberate utterance: no ambient inference, no gaze or dwell logging, no telemetry. This is also the line between authoring and capture-and-replay: an authored record can carry intent and dependencies no sensor recovers, and it contains only what someone chose to say. The system removes only the addressing effort, recipient, referent, and app switch, never authorship. That is the privacy stance: capture of chosen speech, not surveillance.

2Memory Graph

The structuring step is a small typed store, in effect an advanced filter over what a room accumulates. Read against the Framing tab, the graph is awareness maintenance made mechanical: its job is to keep each arriver's picture of the room current, correct, and scoped to them. Each mechanism has a theory link:

MechanismJobTheory link
Supersedesstate, not log: a place shows what is true nowcurrency: awareness "must be maintained and kept up to date" (G&G, p. 416); the record tracks the room, not the room's history of announcements
Depends_Onprerequisites surface first, "read this first"read-before-act coupling; a dependency is future-directed information no perception recovers (G&G, p. 421)
Replies_Tothreads that span days and peopleevent and action history: how the current state came to be stays answerable behind it
Confirmed_byone-tap "seen, will comply"reverse awareness, for the leaver: the author's picture of whether the note landed; the one move where the arriver speaks back into the record
Audiencebroadcast, directed, or project; each arriver hears only what is for themthe readers dimension: one known reader, whoever comes next, or everyone
Routinga project memory follows the work to its next placespatial scope: awareness that must arrive somewhere other than where it was spoken
Triage + salience"anything for me?" returns a few ordered items; the rest stays quieta picture small enough to act on: warnings first, relevance next, silence otherwise
Accumulation + historythe reviewable record behind every current statethe history elements: how, when, and who remain answerable after supersession

Read as maintenance jobs: currency (supersedes), ordering (depends_on), history (replies_to, accumulation), scope (audience, routing, triage), and one return channel (confirmed_by). The value is the graph's position in the loop, not the data model, which a plain table could hold. The hard part is building it from messy in-flow speech.
